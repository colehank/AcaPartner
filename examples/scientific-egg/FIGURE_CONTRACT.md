# Figure contract

All figures depict a synthetic two-node demonstration model, not measured eggs or a reproduction of the cited paper. Python/matplotlib is the repository's existing scientific-execution track. No stock imagery or generated biological photograph is used.

| Figure | Question and claim | Complementary panels | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do the protocols generate distinct white/yolk histories in the toy model? | a: white response; b: delayed yolk response. One declared reference egg per method, not a population mean. | temperature_traces.csv |
| 2 | Does an acceptable mean imply paired success? It does not. | a: all assessable paired texture observations and target rectangle; b: actual paired counts with nominal Wilson 95% intervals. Four missing white readings cannot be plotted and remain in denominators. | classified_samples.csv; method_summary.csv |
| 3 | Is the apparent advantage stable? Batch heterogeneity and criterion sensitivity bound it. | a: batch-specific joint rates (4 generated process groups, n=30/method/batch); b: recomputed rates under explicit white thresholds. | batch_summary.csv; threshold_sensitivity.csv |
| S1 | Where does periodic cooking fail or succeed in the model? | a/b: paired-success counts by size and initial temperature for soft-boiled and periodic protocols, n=20/cell. | condition_summary.csv |

Each is an equal-width quantitative grid, 180 x 79 mm, editable PDF/SVG text and 450 dpi PNG. Main text >=8 pt, no glyph <5 pt. Comparable panel alignment tolerance 1.5 pt; save measured axes and review collision audits. Curves in Fig.1 are individual references with no uncertainty claim. Wilson intervals elsewhere are descriptive egg-level binomial intervals that ignore within-batch dependence; generated batches are shown separately and no real-world significance claim or p-value is made. Full scripts, untouched raw rows, denominators, missing IDs and threshold definitions ship with the plots.
